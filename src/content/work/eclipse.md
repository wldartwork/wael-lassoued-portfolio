Eclipse is a pair of solar eclipse glasses designed, produced and sold by WLD Artwork for the 2026 solar eclipse. Two editions: a white frame with a sun and crescent, and a navy frame with a gold-foil moon and stars.

The object was designed end to end: frame, pattern, print and finishing, then brought to market on Amazon and TikTok Shop, with a customised edition for cities and municipalities.

Product design, packaging, art direction and launch. Over 100,000 pairs sold.
