La Table d'Or is a bistronomy restaurant at the gates of Paris, where French culinary tradition meets the warmth of Oriental influences: quality produce, the art of the flame, the pleasure of sharing.

The identity is built on a deliberate tension between refinement and warmth, a world that feels premium without feeling cold. Logo, colour palette and typography, menus, website and social content were designed as one system, for a restaurant that holds its own without needing to shout.

Brand identity, art direction, print, web and social media.
