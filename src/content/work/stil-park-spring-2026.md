Spring 2026 campaign for Stil Park, produced entirely with directed AI generation. A lone figure in the Highlands wind, cable knit and bare moorland, the horse of the house grazing in the distance.

Built like a real shoot: casting, location, light and lens defined upfront, then a set of stills and a short film consistent enough to run across social, web and in-store screens. The galloping horse of the identity returns on the back of the piece.

Art direction, image and film generation, post-production and editing, on the Stil Park identity system.
