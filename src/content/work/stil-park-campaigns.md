Two campaigns for Stil Park, produced entirely with directed AI generation: a man and his dog on a mountain ridge, a younger figure alone in the Highlands wind. Knitwear, landscape, silence.

Each campaign was built like a real shoot: casting, location, light and lens defined upfront, then a set of stills and short films consistent enough to run across social, web and in-store screens. The galloping horse of the identity returns on the back of each piece.

Art direction, image and film generation, post-production and editing, on the Stil Park identity system.
