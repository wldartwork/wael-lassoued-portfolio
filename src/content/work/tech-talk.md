Tech&Talk is an internal video media created at Louis Vuitton Malletier to give a voice to the people behind the house's technology and transformation projects. Short formats, real conversations, a tone that is closer to a talk show than to a corporate update.

The identity turns the microphone cable into the signature: two mics, one line, the name written in a single stroke. A blue and orange palette, bold typography and a set of animated idents carry the series across the house's internal channels.

Media concept, visual identity, motion and launch campaign. Episodes and interviews are internal content and are not shown here.
